About This Tool

This PEM decoder parses X.509 certificates entirely in your browser. The certificate never leaves your machine — there's no network request when you click Decode.

It extracts the subject, issuer, validity period, serial number, signature algorithm, public key information, Subject Alternative Names (SANs), and computes SHA-1 and SHA-256 fingerprints.

Need to generate a self-signed certificate? Use our free generator — same privacy guarantees.

Self-signed certificates are intended for development and testing only. Do not use them in production.
